Spendline Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Spendline ("the app") is an expense tracker where you type or speak entries such as "lunch 500" or "ali ko 1000 diye". This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data stored on your phone

Your entries, groups, categories and settings are stored in a database on your device. If you never turn on Backup & sync, this data never leaves your phone.

Optional Backup & sync

If you turn on Backup & sync and sign in with Google, the app stores the following under your own account only:

  • Your email address (from Google sign-in) to identify your account.
  • Your entries and groups in a private Firebase (Google Cloud Firestore) database. Security rules allow only you to read or write your data.
  • A spreadsheet named "Spendline" created in your own Google Drive, using the limited drive.file permission, which lets the app access only files it created.

Microphone

If you tap the microphone button, audio is processed by your phone's speech-recognition service to turn speech into text. Spendline does not record or store audio.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show ads, build advertising profiles, or share your data with third parties. The app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Optional on-device AI

If you enable Smart parsing, a language model is downloaded to your phone and runs entirely on the device. Your text is not sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

Turn off Backup & sync and delete entries in the app to remove local data; uninstalling the app removes everything on the device. To delete your cloud copy, email the address below and we will remove it, or delete the "Spendline" spreadsheet from your Google Drive.

Children

Spendline is not directed at children under 13.
